FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What are the timings of Wellcrysta Dental in Chinchwad?

Monday to Saturday, 9:30 AM to 2:00 PM and 5:00 PM to 10:00 PM. The clinic is closed on Sunday.

How much does a dental consultation cost?

The consultation fee is ₹300. Treatment charges are explained after examination; indicative ranges are on our treatment charges page.

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments are recommended so we can keep waiting time short. For tooth pain, call or WhatsApp and we will try to see you the same day.

Which areas do you serve?

Patients visit us from Chinchwad, Akurdi, Nigdi, Pradhikaran, Chikhli, Ravet and the wider Pimpri-Chinchwad area.

Can I pay for treatment in instalments?

EMI options may be available for larger treatment plans such as implants and braces. Ask the front desk for details.
